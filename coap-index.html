COAP | Da'awatul-Rusul — Calling of All Prophets, Call of the Messengers

Reviving and Sharing The Truth according to the understanding of the righteous predecessors (Salaf al-Ṣāliḥah)

COAP | Da'awatul-Rusul is a non-profit, community‑based initiative whose name carries a prophetic message. COAP stands for Calling of All Prophets, and Da'awatul‑Rusul is Arabic for Call of the Messengers. Together, the name expresses one universal invitation: to return to the original truth that every prophet — from Adam to Noah, Abraham to Moses, Jesus to Muhammad (peace be upon them all) — brought to humanity. The organization’s core mission is simple yet profound: Reviving and Sharing The Truth — not as newly invented ideas, but as understood and lived by the first three generations (Salaf al-Ṣāliḥah).

The truth that all prophets called upon is not a new message. It is the oldest and most essential call in human history: to know, love, and worship Allāh alone, and to live a life of justice, mercy, and sincerity. Reviving this truth means restoring the pure, original teachings of Islam exactly as Allāh revealed them and exactly as His prophets delivered them — without changes, additions, or distortions.

In practical terms, this includes three main elements. First, Tawḥīd — the belief that Allāh is One and only He deserves to be worshipped, without any partners, idols, or false gods. Second, following the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah — the Qur'an as Allāh's final book, and the Sunnah as the way of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). Third, removing falsehood, misconceptions, and innovations — gently clearing away incorrect ideas and religious practices that distance people from true Islam.

A key part of reviving the truth is following the understanding of the first three generations (Salaf al-Ṣāliḥah). These are the Companions of the Prophet (Ṣaḥābah), their immediate successors (Tābi‘ūn), and the successors of those successors (Tābi‘ al-Tābi‘īn). The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) praised them, saying: "The best of my community are my generation, then those who follow them, then those who follow them." COAP | Da'awatul-Rusul therefore adopts the interpretation of the Qur'an and Sunnah as understood by these righteous predecessors, avoiding later innovations and divisions.

Our Core Methodology

The prophetic approach to reviving truth

Tawḥīd – The Oneness of Allāh

The central call of every prophet: to worship Allāh alone without any partner, idol, or intermediary. Tawḥīd is the foundation of faith and the key to eternal success. Our dawah emphasizes clarifying this pure monotheism, freeing people from shirk (associating partners with Allāh) and guiding them to sincere devotion.

As Allāh says: "And We sent never a messenger before you except that We revealed to him that there is no deity except Me, so worship Me." (Qur'an 21:25)

Following Qur'an & Authentic Sunnah

The Qur'an is Allāh's final revelation, preserved without any alteration. The Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) explains and applies the Qur'an in daily life. We reject all innovations (bid'ah) and false teachings that contradict these two primary sources. Our invitation is built upon authentic knowledge, not cultural traditions or personal opinions.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "I have left among you two things; you will never go astray as long as you hold fast to them: the Book of Allāh and my Sunnah."

Understanding of the Salaf al-Ṣāliḥah

The best generations – the Companions, their successors, and the next generation – understood Islam perfectly. We adopt their methodology in creed, worship, and character. By following the Salaf, we avoid modern divisions and return to the pure, original Islam. This includes patience, mercy, and calling others with wisdom, not extremism or negligence.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best of my community are my generation, then those who follow them, then those who follow them." (Bukhari & Muslim)
